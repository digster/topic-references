## Link rot and host quirks found (the reusable part)
- **`incompleteideas.net` is HTTP-only.** HTTPS fails at TLS, and `WebFetch` upgrades HTTP→HTTPS,
  so it 503s on *both* the Sutton & Barto book page and *The Bitter Lesson*. `curl` over plain
  `http://` returns 200 for both. Two rows on this page therefore ship as `http://` links
  intentionally — do not "fix" them to HTTPS. Free full PDF is `book/RLbook2020.pdf` (73 MB).
- **`nature.com` 503s to `WebFetch`** but is fine via `curl` (redirects to a
  `?error=cookies_not_supported` URL and still serves the title). Add it to the
  `oreilly.com` / `dl.acm.org` / `science.org` list of hosts to check with curl instead.
- **`academic.oup.com` 403s to `curl`** even with a browser UA (Cloudflare), but `WebFetch` reads
  it fine. The inverse of the two above — when one tool 403/503s, try the other before dropping a
  link.
- **`science.org` still 403s** (Cloudflare interstitial) — the AlphaZero *Science* paper was left
  out rather than linked unverified.
- **`deepmind.google/learning-resources/…` now redirects to the DeepMind homepage** — David
  Silver's RL course page is gone. The YouTube playlist
  `PLqYmG7hTraZDM-OYHWgPebj2MfCFzFObQ` ("DeepMind x UCL | Introduction to Reinforcement Learning
  2015") is the live replacement. Careful: `PLqYmG7hTraZDVH599EItlEWsUOsJbAodm` is the *Deep
  Learning* series, not RL.
- **`aima.cs.berkeley.edu/courses.html` 404s** — the AIMA site's course directory is dead. The
  root, the exercises site and the errata are all fine.
- **YouTube `oembed` does not support channel or `@handle` URLs** — returns 404/non-JSON even for
  live channels. Verify a channel with a plain `curl` + `<title>` check instead; `oembed` is only
  for `watch?v=` and `playlist?list=`.
- **`3blue1brown.com/topics/neural-networks` served homepage content** when fetched — ambiguous,
  so it was dropped rather than guessed at.
- **Scanned PDFs defeat title checks.** `WebFetch` on the UMBC Turing mirror reported only the
  Word metadata (`TuringTest.doc`), and Brooks' `representation.pdf` has no extractable text at
  all. Two workarounds used: decompress the PDF streams in Python and grep the operators for the
  opening line, and check the author's own publications index for the filename → title mapping
  (that is how Brooks 1991 was confirmed).
- Turing 1950: two free mirrors exist. UMBC's misprints the citation as *Mind 49*; Oxford's
  (`cs.ox.ac.uk/activities/ieg/e-library/sources/t_article.pdf`) has the correct *Mind 59,
  433-460* and 21 pages. Shipped the Oxford one, with the paywalled journal version linked
  alongside.
- Dropped for unverifiable provenance: Minsky's *Steps Toward Artificial Intelligence*
  (`courses.csail.mit.edu/6.803/pdf/steps.pdf` is a live 6 MB PDF but appears on no index page,
  so its identity could not be confirmed).
