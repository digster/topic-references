## The cross-link work (the reusable part)
The convention was **never written down** — it existed only in the shipped pages. Now in
`ARCHITECTURE.md` (a "why" section) and `CLAUDE.md` (a contract subsection). The four invariants
that were being followed by imitation:

1. Bare sibling filename (`href="transformers.html"`), never `./` or `../topics/`.
2. **No `target="_blank" rel="noopener"`** — the one attribute-level difference from every other
   link on the site.
3. No `level` pill.
4. `— on this site` verbatim, and the rows always come last in Further Reading.

Plus the new rule: **symmetry inside a family**, not across the whole site.
`data-structures-and-algorithms` and `starting-a-new-bank` stay isolated on purpose.

**The gap that existed before this session:** AI, LLM and gen-AI each linked to all siblings;
`machine-learning` linked to 2 and `transformers` to 1. Reciprocal-only additions never fix an
asymmetry, they just don't add new ones — so it had been compounding since the transformers page
was written.

**Rename gotcha worth remembering:** renaming a page is *not* three edits. Four sibling pages
carried the old title as cross-link **link text**, and `README.md` had it once more. Always
`grep -rn '<old title>'` across `*.html` and `*.md` after a rename, not just the page itself.
