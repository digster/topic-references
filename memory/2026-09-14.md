## Scope decision (made, not asked)

One page, not two. The prompt says "a new topic" (singular), and the two movements are mutually
defining — post-structuralism *is* structuralism's premise turned on structuralism — so two pages
would each have had to re-explain the other. Ordered as a single argument rather than
alphabetically: Saussure's sign → the method exported to myth/advertising/the unconscious →
Derrida's 1966 lecture as the hinge → Foucault on discourse and power.

**🔧 Tools & Interactive omitted** — nothing interactive exists here that isn't padding. Rule 5.

## The page is deliberately isolated

There is a real intellectual line from Saussure's "a system of differences with no positive terms"
to distributional semantics and word embeddings, and it was tempting to wire this page to
`large-language-models`. It fails `CLAUDE.md`'s actual test: a reader studying structuralism is not
thereby ready for the LLM page, and vice versa. **An intellectual resemblance is not a next step.**
So the site now has three isolated pages: `data-structures-and-algorithms`,
`structuralism-and-post-structuralism`, and (but for its one DeFi edge) `starting-a-new-bank`.

## Link rot and host quirks found (add to the running list)

- **Hosts that need a real browser** (both `curl` *and* `WebFetch` fail): `global.oup.com` (returns
  **202 with a 0-byte body** — a bot challenge that looks like neither an error nor a page, so it
  will silently pass a naive status check), `press.jhu.edu`, `us.macmillan.com`,
  `manchesteruniversitypress.co.uk`, `jscholarship.library.jhu.edu`, `www.library.jhu.edu`,
  `jstor.org`, `openlibrary.org`, `open.edu`. All six shipped links on these hosts were verified in
  the in-app browser.
- **`manchesteruniversitypress.co.uk` defeated even the browser** (persistent Cloudflare
  interstitial) — Peter Barry's *Beginning Theory* was dropped rather than shipped unverified.
- **Soft-404s that return 200: check the byte size.** `www9.georgetown.edu/faculty/irvinem/theory/…`
  served an identical **16,349-byte** "Martin Irvine, Georgetown University" page for two completely
  different paths. Same size across unrelated URLs is the tell. Martin Irvine's theory pages, widely
  cited for Derrida and Barthes full texts, are **gone**.
- **A 200 with the wrong content, again.** Two cases this session:
  - `jstor.org/stable/536101`, recalled as Lévi-Strauss's *The Structural Study of Myth*, is
    actually **"Traditional Ballads from Utah"** (JAF 64/251, 1951). No verified URL for the myth
    essay was found, so it is **not on the page**.
  - `cornellpress.cornell.edu/book/9780801474040/on-deconstruction/` — a plausible ISBN one digit
    off — serves a real 200 page for ***The Familial State* by Julia Adams**. The correct ISBN is
    **9780801474057**.
- **Verify scanned PDFs by rendering page 1, not by metadata.** `pdftoppm -png -r 70 -f 1 -l 1
  file.pdf out` then Read the image. This is how two links were *rejected*:
  `web.english.upenn.edu/~cavitch/pdf-library/Foucault_Author.pdf` is a scan of *Aesthetics, Method,
  and Epistemology* (The New Press, 1998) and `…/Jakobson_Linguistics_and_Poetics.pdf` is a scan of
  *Language in Literature* (Belknap/Harvard, 1987) — i.e. whole in-copyright **books**, not
  standalone essays. Note the PDF `/Title` on the Jakobson scan was just `Jakobson-Linguistics-and-
  Poetics.pdf`, which would have "confirmed" it; the metadata echoes the filename and proves nothing.
- **Working line on free full texts** (applied consistently, worth keeping): link single canonical
  *essays* on university hosts, and legitimate public archives (marxists.org, archive.org, an
  author's own page); do **not** link full-book or full-chapter scans of in-print books — use the
  publisher page and flag the cost instead.
- **archive.org: use the metadata API to tell free-to-read from borrow-only.**
  `https://archive.org/metadata/<id>` → `access-restricted-item: true` means lending only.
  `courseingenerall00saus` (Saussure, 1959 Baskin) has **no** restriction and a public
  `_djvu.txt` — genuinely free full text. `courseingenerall00ferd` (1986) is borrow-only. Likewise
  borrow-only: `ofgrammatology0000derr`, `writingdifferenc0000derr`, `structuralanthro0000levi`.
  The advancedsearch JSON endpoint did **not** work from here; the metadata endpoint did.
- **Derrida's "Structure, Sign and Play" has one good free copy:**
  `www.eve.gd/derrida/1966-StructureSignAndPlay.pdf` — Martin Paul Eve's own typeset edition of the
  Bass translation, 11pp, GFDL, up since 2009. Confirmed with `pdftotext -f 1 -l 1`. Everything else
  found was Scribd/academia.edu/Course Hero or dead.
- **Unique find worth reusing:** JHU Sheridan Libraries digitised the **1966 "Languages of Criticism
  and the Sciences of Man" symposium** — actual audio of Derrida delivering the lecture *plus the
  hostile Q&A the published essay omits* — at `jscholarship.library.jhu.edu/handle/1774.2/66902`.
  The handle is only discoverable from the library blog post; the repository's own collection IDs
  404 to a login page.
- **Purdue OWL's "Literary Theory and Schools of Criticism" pages are gone** — every path under
  `owl.purdue.edu/owl/subject_specific_writing/writing_in_literature/literary_theory_and_schools_of_criticism/`
  404s. Do not link them from memory; `iep.utm.edu/literary/` is the live replacement.
- **No SEP or IEP entry exists for Saussure, Lévi-Strauss, or "post-structuralism" as such.**
  `plato.stanford.edu/entries/{saussure,levi-strauss,structuralism-linguistics}` and
  `iep.utm.edu/{saussure,poststru}` all 404. The live ones are SEP `derrida`, `foucault`, `lacan`,
  `postmodernism` and IEP `literary`, `derrida`, `foucault`, `lacweb`.
- **`philosophizethis.org` renders an empty `<title>` and a literal `<br/` inside `og:title`** — a
  Squarespace quirk. A curl title check reports nothing even on a good page; confirm in the browser.
- `youtube.com/oembed` remains the reliable ID check. Confirmed: `rkDb9Nt1EBQ` and `P2eb52fUgTk`
  are **Tom Nicholas**, `HKJlSY0DBBA` is **Then & Now** — these are two different channels despite
  the near-identical "WTF?" explainer format, and are easy to attribute to the wrong one.
