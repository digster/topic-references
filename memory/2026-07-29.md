## Verification notes (this is the part worth re-reading)
- **A 200 status is not proof the page exists.** `rbi.org.in/commonperson/English/Scripts/
  BankLicensing.aspx` returns **HTTP 200 serving an error page** ("The file you are looking for is
  not available"). Only a content fetch caught it. Replaced with the real press release for the
  *Guidelines for 'on tap' Licensing of Universal Banks in the Private Sector* (prid=37658, 1 Aug
  2016), content-verified.
- **`github.com` returns 403 to `curl` from this container** — bot blocking, not dead links. All four
  GitHub URLs (`nanoGPT`, `nanochat`, `TransformerLens`, `rasbt/LLMs-from-scratch`) were verified by
  `WebFetch` instead.
- **`youtube.com` returns 429 to `curl`** — rate limiting. All video IDs and the two playlists were
  verified through the `oembed` endpoint, which returns title + channel and is not rate-limited.
- **`huggingface.co/learn/llm-course/` 503s on `WebFetch`** but serves 200 to `curl`; kept on the
  strength of the `curl` check plus it being the canonical URL.
- **Deliberately excluded:** Bishop's *Deep Learning: Foundations and Concepts* — `bishopbook.com`
  answers 202 behind a challenge and the content could not be confirmed. *Digital Bank* (Skinner,
  2014) — dropped as stale, superseded by *Bank 4.0* and *Banking On It*. Treasury Prime — cut as
  redundant once Column, Unit and Stripe Treasury were all on the page.
- **nanoGPT is deprecated** in favour of Karpathy's `nanochat` (Nov 2025). Kept nanoGPT as the
  teaching repo it still is, with the successor linked in its note.
