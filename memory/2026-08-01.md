## The cross-link graph is deliberately incomplete

The obvious move was to copy the AI family and build a complete graph — 21 pairs, 42 rows.
That would have violated `CLAUDE.md`'s actual rule, which is *"only link pages that are
actually a next step for each other — a shared field is not enough."* NFTs↔ZK, DeFi↔ZK,
DAOs↔ZK and L2↔DAOs all fail that test.

Final shape — **17 of 21 pairs**, 34 rows:

- `blockchain-and-cryptocurrency` is the hub → links to all six siblings (it is every other
  page's prerequisite).
- `ethereum-and-smart-contracts` → DeFi, NFTs, L2, ZK, DAOs.
- `decentralized-finance` → NFTs, L2, DAOs.
- `nfts-and-digital-ownership` → L2, DAOs.
- `layer-2-and-scaling` → ZK (the strongest edge in the family).

Row counts: Blockchain 6, Ethereum 6, DeFi 6 (incl. bank), NFTs 5, L2 5, DAOs 4, ZK 3.

**One edge leaves the family:** `decentralized-finance` ↔ `starting-a-new-bank`. DeFi
reimplements deposits, lending and payment rails without a licence, so each is a genuine
next step from the other. The bank page's `Last updated:` was bumped to 2026-08-01 for its
new row. `data-structures-and-algorithms` stays fully isolated.

`ARCHITECTURE.md` was updated for this — previously the AI complete graph was the only
worked example, which read as "families must be complete". It now states that symmetry ≠
completeness, and documents both families' shapes plus the cross-family edge. `README.md`
was left alone (enumerates neither topics nor a count), consistent with the last five
topic-add sessions.
